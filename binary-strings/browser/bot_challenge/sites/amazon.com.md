## Bot detection

- The AI-agent wall says “Continued access by an unauthorized AI agent violates
  Amazon's Conditions of Use”, with a “Continue” button. It is a refusal, not a
  check: do not click “Continue”; report it at once, quoting the page.
- The text CAPTCHA, “Enter the characters you see below” over an image of
  characters, is a bot check. Resume the task once it clears.
- Do not fill in product details you could not read.
