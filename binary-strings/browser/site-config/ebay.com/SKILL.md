# eBay instructions

## Searching

- Search with “Search for anything”, optionally narrowed by its category menu,
  or open `/sch/i.html?_nkw=<encoded query>`.
- On a listing, check condition, seller, shipping and the selected variation.

## Buying

- “Buy It Now”, an auction bid and “Make offer” are different operations. An
  offer or bid is not the same as adding a fixed-price item to the cart.
- Read the cart or confirmation after an action. Never repeat a bid, offer or
  purchase because its confirmation was slow.

## Selling

- “List an item” suggests catalogue matches; choosing one can prefill item
  details. Verify condition, category, variation and shipping before
  publishing, and never resubmit a listing whose result is uncertain.
- Active listings live in Seller Hub (“Manage active listings”).

## Signing in

1. Enter “Mobile number, email, or username” and “Continue”. eBay may send a
   one-time code by text or email at this step.
2. The next page shows a password field labelled for that account (“Password
   for …”). “Text me a code” switches to a code instead; keep the method you
   chose.

“Stay signed in” is ticked by default. Google, Apple and Facebook sign-in are
separate methods.

## Bot detection

- eBay checks automated browsing often. Its check is titled “Pardon Our
  Interruption...” and reads “Checking your browser before you access eBay.”
  and “Your browser will redirect to your requested content shortly.” It is
  meant to clear on its own; wait for the redirect instead of reloading.
- A search can also return eBay's error page, “SORRY” and “Something went
  wrong on our end”. It is not an empty result set.
- When a check does not clear, treat it as a bot challenge.
