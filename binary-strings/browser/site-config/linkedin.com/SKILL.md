# LinkedIn instructions

## Page setup

- Dismiss sign-in prompts and Premium offers, such as “Dismiss Job search
  smarter with Premium”, when they cover the page. Never start a Premium
  trial.

## Finding jobs

- Search from “I'm looking for…”. Filters such as “Date posted” apply to the
  current results.
- The job tracker at `/jobs-tracker/` sorts jobs into “Saved”, “In Progress”,
  “Applied”, “Interview” and “Archived”.

## Applying

- “Easy Apply to this job” is LinkedIn's own application. “Apply on company
  website” opens the employer's flow; continue on the new page.
- Check the attached résumé and every required question. Experience, work
  authorization and sponsorship answers must come from the facts you were
  given, never from defaults.
- “Save the job” is not applying. Report a submission in `browser.report` only
  after LinkedIn shows the application receipt.

## Signing in

- The sign-in form takes “Email or phone” and “Password”, then “Sign in”.
  Google and Apple are separate methods, and “Sign in using another account”
  switches accounts.

## Bot detection

- Bot checks appear occasionally. Treat one as a bot challenge and do not
  resubmit the sign-in or application while it is showing.
