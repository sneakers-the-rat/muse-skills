# Rakuten instructions

## Searching

- Search with “キーワード検索” (“キーワードから探す” on shop and result pages).
  Results mix offers from different shops.
- Narrow results by delivery prefecture (“お届け先”), free shipping (“送料無料”)
  and delivery date (“お届け日”). Sold-out items appear only with
  “売り切れを含む”.

## Product pages

- Open the chosen shop's product and confirm size, colour and quantity before
  “かごに追加” (add to cart). “購入手続きへ” goes straight to purchase.
- Compare the item price, shipping and delivery terms separately from points
  and coupons. Points earned do not reduce the amount paid.
- “ページが表示できません” means the item page was removed or moved. The page
  sends you to the Rakuten top page after a few seconds.

## Cart

- Recheck the shop and items in the cart (“買い物かご”). Sign-in and the
  shopping confirmation page are not a completed order; placed orders appear
  under “購入履歴”.

## Bot detection

- Rakuten blocks automated visits occasionally, mainly on search. The block
  page reads “アクセスが集中し、ページを閲覧しにくい状態になっております” (the
  page is hard to view because of heavy traffic) with a Reference number.
  Despite the wording, it is an access block, not congestion.
- Treat it as a bot challenge. Reloading the search does not clear it.
