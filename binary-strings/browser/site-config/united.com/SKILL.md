# United instructions

## Page setup

- Close the “Sign in for the best experience” popover and promotional banners
  (“Close banner”) before using the booking form.

## Searching for flights

1. Open “Search and book a flight”, then choose “Round Trip” or “One Way”. The
   hotel, car and vacation tabs run different searches.
2. Fill “From” and “To” and choose the matching airport options.
3. Choose the full date range in the date picker; “Next month” only moves the
   calendar. Recheck the date summary.
4. Set “Travelers” and the cabin class, then use “Find flights”. The header
   “Search” searches the site, not flights.

## Fares and miles

- Results default to “Show price in: Money”. “Miles” and “Money + Miles” show
  award prices only to a signed-in MileagePlus account.
- Compare the selected fare's baggage, change and seat terms. A flight
  selection or payment review is not a ticketed reservation.
- A seat map reached before booking is for viewing only.

## Existing trips

- “View and manage current trips” asks for the confirmation number (“Please
  enter your 6 digit confirmation number”) and the reservation's last name,
  then “Next”.
- If the form empties itself after you submit it, with no error, United's bot
  protection refused the lookup. Treat it as a bot challenge rather than
  resubmitting.

## Signing in

- “Sign in” opens the MileagePlus sign-in. A generic “Something went wrong” can
  appear before the password step; it does not mean the credentials are wrong.
  It is United's bot protection: treat it as a bot challenge rather than
  retrying the sign-in.

## Bot detection

- United rarely shows a CAPTCHA, but its protection often acts silently: a
  submitted form resets to empty, or sign-in fails with “Something went wrong”.
- Treat a visible challenge or a silent reset as a bot challenge. Resubmitting
  the same form does not get past it; report it only after the bot-check agent
  could not.
