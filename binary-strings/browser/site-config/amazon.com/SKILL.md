# Amazon instructions

## Storefront and location

- Use the storefront for the delivery country, and set “Deliver to” before
  comparing prices or availability. Never carry a price or stock status from
  one storefront to another.
- Search accepts a product name, model number or ASIN. `/s?k=<encoded query>`
  opens a search; `/dp/<ASIN>` opens a product you have already identified.

## Product pages

- Choose size, colour, pack count and condition before adding. A variant change
  can change the seller, price and delivery date, so read the offer again after
  each choice.
- In the buying-options drawer every “Add to Cart from seller … and price …”
  belongs to one offer. Use the button for the offer you chose, not the first
  match.
- Buy once unless the task says otherwise. Subscribe & Save, free trials and
  protection plans are separate decisions, and “Buy Now” skips the cart.

## Cart

- Read the cart after adding. Adding the same item again raises the quantity of
  its existing line.
- The cart, order review and payment-method pages are not an order
  confirmation.

## Signing in

- Shopping sign-in uses `/ap/signin`: email or mobile number, “Continue”, then
  the password on its own page. A one-time code, when Amazon asks for one, goes
  in a single field.
- AWS shares this site but has its own two-step sign-in: the email address,
  “Next”, then the password on the following page.

## Bot detection

- The AI-agent wall says “Continued access by an unauthorized AI agent violates
  Amazon's Conditions of Use”, with a “Continue” button. It is a refusal, not a
  check: do not click “Continue”; report it at once, quoting the page.
- The text CAPTCHA, “Enter the characters you see below” over an image of
  characters, is a bot check. Resume the task once it clears.
- Do not fill in product details you could not read.
