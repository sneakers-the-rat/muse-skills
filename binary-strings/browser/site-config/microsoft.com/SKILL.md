# Microsoft instructions

## Finding the right flow

- The Store, Microsoft 365 sign-up, careers and account sign-in run on
  different pages and hosts. Identify the one the task needs before acting.

## Store

- Configure pages walk through numbered steps; a later step such as “Protect
  your new XBOX” unlocks only after the earlier ones. Add a protection plan
  only when the task asks.
- In the cart, set the quantity with the item's “Quantity selection” box.
  “Save For Later” moves an item out of the purchase; check the remaining items
  and total before paying.
- Checkout can offer “Check out with an account” and “Check out as a guest”.
  Follow the path the task asks for.

## Microsoft 365 sign-up

- signup.microsoft.com runs in three stages: “Subscription & account details”,
  “Sign-in details”, then “Add payment, confirm & complete order”.
- “Verify your address” offers the entered address and a suggested one, which
  can carry a different ZIP. Choose deliberately, then press “Use this
  address”.

## Signing in

- Sign-in can ask for an account type or a verification method after “Next”.
  Read each new form before entering anything.
- “Confirm password” belongs to creating or changing a password, never to an
  ordinary sign-in.

## Bot detection

- Bot checks appear occasionally. Treat one as a bot challenge and do not
  resubmit the form while it is showing.
