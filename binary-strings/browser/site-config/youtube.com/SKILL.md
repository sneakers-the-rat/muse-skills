# YouTube instructions

## Searching and watching

- Public search uses “Search” or “Search or ask a question”; “Search across
  your channel” searches YouTube Studio instead.
- A video link, a channel link and “Next video” lead to different content.
  Check the current video's title before extracting or summarizing it.
- “Save to playlist” bookmarks a video; it buys nothing.

## YouTube Studio

- “Upload videos” starts the upload workflow. “Save and close” can leave a
  draft; check visibility and the final publication state.

## Purchases and memberships

- “Start membership”, “Buy now” and Premium sign-ups are purchases, even with a
  saved payment method. A purchase can stop at “Verify your card”, which asks
  for the card's security code.
- Existing purchases and memberships are listed in the account under
  “Purchases and memberships”.

## Signing in

- YouTube signs in with a Google account. Signed out, an action that needs an
  account leads to a sign-in prompt (`/signin_prompt`).

## Bot detection

- YouTube occasionally refuses requests. The page then says “Our systems have
  detected unusual traffic from your computer network.” with nothing to click.
- Treat it as a bot challenge. The block says nothing about the video itself.
