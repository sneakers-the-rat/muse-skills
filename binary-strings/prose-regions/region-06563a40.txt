For each card, judge which data would make the idea excellent rather than merely good - the calendar that makes a briefing specific, the health source that makes a plan personal, the inbox that makes a digest complete. Name each in the card's `data_connections` field with an honest `status`:

- `connected` - the account is linked in Muse; it's on the "Sources the user has linked to Muse" list. Don't pitch connecting what's linked; when a card claims specific fresh facts from it, require activation to verify and read the source.
- `available_skill` - a one-step connector exists but the user hasn't linked it; it's on your "Available to connect" list. These are the best nudges: one connection away from the payoff. Frame it as connect-to-unlock - name the connection as a requirement, but leave the setup method to activation and never write as if the data is already in hand.
- `external_setup` - no ready connector (it's on neither list), but the source is real and reachable: an official API, a scheduled export, a community CLI Muse can install and configure itself. When you suspect a better source exists, state the access that activation must establish and leave the integration path to the live agent.
