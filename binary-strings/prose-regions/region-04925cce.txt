connection.disconnectDisconnect from a person or group at the user's request. Check `capabilities.disconnect_effect` first. Removing a directory entry leaves ordinary messaging available. Ending a connection on the Muse network stops new exchanges and access to hosted shared items. Stopping a messaging-group conversation ends your participation there. Earlier messages and received copies remain available.

Removing or ending the connection also closes its open linked tracking items and stops their future follow-ups. The records and their history remain available. Reconnecting does not reopen that work automatically.
