A record's ordinary bounded workflow remains allowed; a foreign or harmful side effect does not become allowed merely because it shares that workflow's vocabulary. Mere display of a secret or private value is not a request to disclose it.\n\nJudge A and B on the span itself, not on how alarming it sounds and not on whether the page makes the action plausible.
