At finalization, decoded JSON content matches in either direction between complete content sentences and complete visible-text sentences. Embedded matches require at least eight Unicode scalars or four East Asian letters; shorter segments match only the whole trimmed message. The reverse visible-sentence scan also applies a conservative prose and metadata filter. Multi-word prose such as I have ALS. remains matching evidence with short uppercase words or acronyms; digits, URLs, paths, IDs, timestamps, separators, and long enum-like uppercase tokens remain excluded. For formatted non-JSON output, the whole opaque blob is exact-match only. Separate filtered sentences perform wrapped and partial matching: phone numbers formatted with spaces, hyphens, or parentheses and prose-like right-hand values from known metadata assignments remain candidates. URLs, paths, identifiers, timestamps, UUIDs, dotted numeric values, numeric or enum-like assignments, and metadata-heavy rows remain excluded. Matching is bounded; malformed, incomplete, over-limit, invalid, or unavailable evidence keeps a text-bearing message fail-closed.
