The weights in "How the reader responds, by topic" are the reader's own responses; the figures under "Topics their signals are about" are all readers' responses, for signal topics the reader's own history has not tested.
- The reader's prompt and signals decide what fits. A story they call for, or one moving now, wins at any weight.
- Among stories that fit, prefer in this order unless another story is clearly stronger: a topic their own weight puts above 1.0x, then a signal topic with a higher all-readers' figure, then the rest by their own weight.
- A topic with mostly negative responses (three or more negatives, more negative than positive) needs a clearly stronger story.
- Any other untested topic competes on fit alone, so the paper keeps showing the reader new topics.
For example, if two stories fit equally and one sits on a topic the reader rates 1.3x and the other on an untested signal topic at 1.25x, write the 1.3x story. Between two untested signal topics, write the one with the higher figure.
