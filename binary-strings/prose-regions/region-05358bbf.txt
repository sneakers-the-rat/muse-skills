verification/health.mdVerify consequential health claims and recommendations in the draft against
reliable medical sources and the user's supplied context. Check evidence for
benefits, risks, contraindications, interactions, and any stated doses or units
when relevant to the advice. Distinguish established evidence from uncertain
findings. Do not treat a general health claim as proof that advice is suitable
for this user. Identify unsupported diagnoses, causal claims, or assurances.
If missing personal context prevents verification, state the limitation so the
resolve phase can qualify or correct the advice without inventing details.
verification/bookings.mdVerify factual claims that support the draft's purchase recommendation or
assessment of a booking option. Check the specific option against the user's
requirements using the applicable dates, location, price, availability,
included amenities, and restrictions. Do not treat a general property of a
venue or provider as proof that the selected option satisfies a requirement.
Distinguish confirmed facts from preferences or judgments. If a consequential
claim is incorrect or cannot be established, identify the correction or
missing evidence so the resolve phase can revise or qualify the recommendation.

For a browser checkout, add or recommend a payment route only when the latest
BrowserTask handoff lists its exact provider ID in
`available_payment_providers`. A wallet connection, saved method, prior use, or
merchant button is not evidence for a new browser payment route. Preserve a
provider the user explicitly requested even when the handoff does not list it.
Do not claim that an unlisted provider will work before the BrowserTask and
runtime validate it.

For a native booking flow, verify a proposed payment route against the booking
skill and the payment methods returned for that flow. Evaluate a completed
payment against the booking tool result. Do not require a BrowserTask handoff
or `available_payment_providers`.

Do not make a purchase or change a reservation.
verification/purchases.mdVerify claims that affect the purchase decision against evidence for the
specific item or service, seller, variant, quantity, and user's requirements.
Check the price and currency, taxes, shipping, mandatory fees, discount
eligibility, and any recurring charges. Distinguish the advertised price from
a confirmed total. Check claimed availability, specifications, delivery dates,
and return or refund terms when they affect the recommendation.
Verify claimed savings against evidence for the comparison price. Do not treat
an expired offer or a price for another variant as the current offer.
If a consequential claim is incorrect or cannot be established, identify the
correction or missing evidence so the resolve phase can revise or qualify it.
Do not make a purchase or change an order or subscription.
