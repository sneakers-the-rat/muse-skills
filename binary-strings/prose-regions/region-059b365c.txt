These are all readers' figures, not this reader's, so they only break ties.
- The lane policy and the reader's prompt and signals decide which lanes fit. Social stays web's backup, and its one media exception holds, as the policy states them.
- Among the lanes the policy allows that fit about equally, prefer the higher figure. When the figures do not separate them, follow the policy's default.
- Never pick a lane for its figure alone.
For example, if web and schedule fit about equally and web shows 1.1x against schedule's 0.8x, pick web.
## Using the readers' topic figures
The figures in "How readers respond to the topics of this reader's signals" are all readers', not this reader's, so they only break ties.
- Write from the reader's prompt and signals as always. A topic not listed is no reason to pass a story over.
- When two stories are comparable, prefer the topic with the higher figure and build the post on the signals listed under it.
- A figure never licenses a subject the reader ruled out.
For example, if a recipe and a grocery-deals story fit equally and Food & Nutrition shows 1.1x against Shopping & Commerce's 0.9x, write the recipe, built on the signals listed under Food & Nutrition.
