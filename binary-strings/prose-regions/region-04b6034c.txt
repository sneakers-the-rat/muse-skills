

Step 1: Identify the topic
Read the post and identify what the story is about as a whole -- the subject, not one detail and not a metaphor. Some subjects share their name with an unrelated everyday thing; when that happens the topic is what the subject IS, not the word it shares. Then pick the icon that represents that subject directly.

Step 2: Find the tightest literal match
From the available icons, pick the one that most specifically represents the topic. Always prefer the tightest icon that still conveys the subject over a broader category icon. A story about golf gets the golf icon, not a generic sports one. A story about hiking in a forest gets the forest icon if it exists, not a generic nature one. Only fall back to a broader category when no tighter match exists in the library.

Step 3: Verify the pick
Before committing, run these four checks.
The guess test: if someone saw only this icon with no other context, could they reasonably guess the post's general topic? If the connection needs explaining, pick something more obvious.
The literal test: does the icon match what the story is ABOUT, or something loosely associated with it? A golf story gets a golf icon, not a basketball. A cybersecurity story gets a lock or a shield, not swiss cheese because of "holes".
The name test: did you pick this icon because its name matches the subject's NAME rather than what the subject is? A story about Apple the technology company does not get the apple, which is fruit; it gets the smartphone, laptop or chip icon. A story about Target the retailer does not get the target. Look up what the icon depicts, not what it is called.
The cleverness test: is the icon a pun, a visual metaphor, or a play on a word in the headline? If yes, it is wrong. A band named Train does not get a train. A story about Taco Bell lettuce does not get a taco. A recall over ball joints does not get a golf ball. A film whose preview frame shows a turtle does not get a turtle. Match the topic, never the wordplay.
If the icon fails any check, go back to step 2 and take the next-best literal match.
