This specialist prepared the cart, entered contact, shipping, and payment details, and submitted the order. Its runtime facts include `purchase`, the recorded purchase outcome (`none`, `not_submitted`, `submitted_unknown`, `placed`, or `failed`), and `payment`. Tool results name checkout progress: `checkout.commit` returning `card_provided` or `payment_input_applied` means payment details were entered or applied, and `checkout.submit` returning `submitted` means the order was submitted.

### failure_stage

- `cart`: reviewing or changing the cart, before any checkout page.
- `checkout_details`: contact, shipping address, delivery options, or traveler details.
- `payment_entry`: choosing a payment method or filling payment fields, before the payment details were complete.
- `card_entered`: payment details were complete or a wallet card was applied, and the order was not yet submitted.
- `order_submitted`: the specialist submitted the payment or order, such as clicking Place order or Pay, and no confirmation followed.
- `confirmation`: the merchant confirmed the order.
- `unknown`: the evidence does not show how far it got.

### failure_reason

- `none`: the merchant confirmed the order, or the specialist finished its assignment without a purchase, such as preparing a cart for review.
- `user_declined`: the user declined the payment approval or told the specialist to stop.
- `bot_or_risk_block`: the site's bot or fraud protection stopped the specialist and it never got past it: a cue under `bot_detection` other than `unexplained_rejection_after_submit`, `silent_no_op_after_submit`, or `session_reset_after_submit` alone, or a site message that attributes the refusal to automated activity, security, or a risk or fraud review. This includes a refusal right after the card was entered or the order was submitted. A challenge the specialist solved before it stopped for another reason is not this reason.
- `card_authentication_failed`: the card issuer's verification, such as 3-D Secure, a bank app approval, or a bank code, failed or was not completed.
- `payment_declined_with_reason`: the merchant or its payment processor declined the payment and stated a reason, such as insufficient funds, an invalid or expired card, a security code or billing address mismatch, or a card type it does not accept.
- `payment_error_generic`: the merchant or its payment processor refused the payment with a message that gives no reason, such as "Your payment could not be processed" or "Please try another payment method".
- `payment_method_unsupported`: the checkout offered no payment method the specialist could use, such as no standard card form, or only a wallet, financing, or in-store option.
- `item_unavailable`: an item was out of stock or unavailable, its quantity was limited, or its price or availability changed.
- `shipping_or_address`: the address was rejected, the merchant does not deliver there, or no delivery option was available.
- `sign_in_required`: checkout required signing in or creating an account, and that did not complete.
- `user_input_needed`: the specialist stopped for a choice or information only the user could give.
- `order_unconfirmed`: the order was submitted, and no confirmation or rejection was observed.
- `site_error`: a site error page or broken page, with no sign of bot or fraud protection.
- `page_unresponsive`: a control did nothing after retries, with no message and no other cue of bot protection.
- `agent_error`: the specialist's own mistake, or it stopped without trying an available next step.
- `other`
- `inconclusive`

A generic payment refusal right after the card was entered is `payment_error_generic` unless the site attributes it to security, risk, or automation, or another bot-detection cue appears with it; record `unexplained_rejection_after_submit` under `bot_detection` either way.

### bot_detection stages

- `none`: no cue was observed.
- `before_payment`: on the cart or checkout details pages.
- `payment_entry`: while the specialist was choosing a payment method or filling payment fields.
- `after_card_entry`: after the payment details were complete, including on or after submitting the payment or order.

This specialist signed in to a site with a saved login, secure entry by the user, a sign-in provider, or a one-time code. The user approves each use of a saved login on a consent card. Tool results from `login.advance` name its progress and the consent outcome, such as `not_authorized` when the user denied consent and `consent_timed_out` when nobody answered.

### failure_stage

- `find_sign_in`: looking for or opening the sign-in page.
- `credential_entry`: entering the account name and password, or choosing a sign-in provider.
- `verification`: a one-time code, two-factor prompt, or account verification step after the credentials.
- `post_sign_in`: the site accepted the credentials and any code, but did not reach a signed-in page.
- `signed_in`: the site showed the specialist signed in.
- `unknown`: the evidence does not show how far it got.

### failure_reason

- `none`: the specialist signed in or confirmed an existing signed-in session.
- `user_declined`: the user denied sign-in consent or told the specialist to stop.
- `bot_or_risk_block`: the site's bot or fraud protection stopped the specialist and it never got past it: a cue under `bot_detection` other than `silent_no_op_after_submit` or `session_reset_after_submit` alone, or a site message that blames automated activity, security, or unusual activity, including an account lock that follows right after sign-in. A challenge the specialist solved before it stopped for another reason is not this reason.
- `credentials_rejected`: the site said the account name or password was wrong.
- `no_saved_login`: there was no saved login for the site and no one supplied credentials.
- `consent_unanswered`: the sign-in consent or secure-entry request went unanswered.
- `verification_unavailable`: a one-time code or verification was required, and no code arrived, the code expired, or the user did not supply it.
- `account_locked_or_restricted`: the site locked, suspended, or restricted the account, or required account recovery, with no sign of bot or fraud protection.
- `provider_sign_in_failed`: signing in through Google, Apple, Amazon, or another provider failed.
- `site_error`: a site error page or broken page, with no sign of bot or fraud protection.
- `page_unresponsive`: a control did nothing after retries, with no message and no other cue of bot protection.
- `agent_error`: the specialist's own mistake, or it stopped without trying an available next step.
- `other`
- `inconclusive`

### bot_detection stages

- `none`: no cue was observed.
- `before_credentials`: before the specialist submitted credentials.
- `after_credentials`: on or after submitting the credentials.
- `verification`: during a one-time code or account verification step.

This specialist tried to get through a bot check or CAPTCHA so the protected page would load. Tool results from `bot_challenge.assess`, `bot_challenge.guide`, and `bot_challenge.recover` name what it found and tried.

### failure_stage

- `assess`: identifying the challenge.
- `solve`: attempting the challenge.
- `post_solve`: the site accepted a solve, but the protected page did not load or a new challenge appeared.
- `cleared`: the protected page loaded.
- `unknown`: the evidence does not show how far it got.

### failure_reason

- `none`: the protected page loaded.
- `hard_block`: a block page or refusal that offered no interactive way through.
- `ip_or_rate_limited`: the site blamed the IP address, network, VPN or proxy, or the request rate.
- `challenge_loop`: after the site accepted a solve, a new challenge appeared at least twice or the protected page never loaded.
- `challenge_unsolved`: the specialist's attempts at the challenge failed.
- `unsupported_challenge`: a challenge the specialist could not attempt, such as an audio-only challenge, a puzzle it could not operate, or a phone or email verification.
- `site_error`: a site error page or broken page that was not a block.
- `agent_error`: the specialist's own mistake, or it stopped without trying an available next step.
- `other`
- `inconclusive`

### bot_detection stages

A challenge this specialist never got past is `challenge_uncleared`, beside any other cue it showed.

- `none`: no cue was observed.
- `page_load`: the first cue was on a challenge or block that appeared when a page loaded.
- `after_action`: the first cue was on a challenge or block that appeared after an action on the page, such as a search, adding to cart, signing in, or submitting a form.

### challenge_vendor

The provider of the challenge, from its branding, widget, or page markers:

- `recaptcha`: Google reCAPTCHA, including "I'm not a robot" and image grids.
- `hcaptcha`: hCaptcha.
- `cloudflare_turnstile`: a Cloudflare Turnstile widget.
- `cloudflare_interstitial`: a Cloudflare "Just a moment…" or "Checking your browser" page.
- `perimeterx`: a HUMAN or PerimeterX "Press & Hold" challenge.
- `datadome`: a DataDome slider or challenge page.
- `arkose`: an Arkose Labs or FunCaptcha puzzle.
- `akamai`: an Akamai challenge or block page.
- `geetest`: a GeeTest slider or puzzle.
- `aws_waf`: an AWS WAF CAPTCHA.
- `site_own`: a challenge the site built itself, such as a text or arithmetic CAPTCHA.
- `other`: a provider not listed here.
- `unknown`: the evidence does not show the provider, or no challenge appeared.
