## purchase_type

Set `purchase_type` to what the checkout was paying for, so purchases can be grouped by kind, such as travel bookings apart from goods. It describes the checkout whatever its outcome, including one that was never submitted.

- `physical_goods`: products shipped or picked up, other than groceries and meals.
- `food_and_groceries`: groceries, meal delivery, or takeout.
- `flight`: airline tickets, including seats, bags, or other extras bought with them.
- `stay`: a hotel or other lodging.
- `car_rental`: a rental car.
- `package`: a flight and a stay booked together, with or without other travel.
- `other_travel`: a train, bus, ferry, cruise, tour, or other travel booking.
- `event_tickets`: tickets to a concert, game, show, movie, or attraction.
- `services`: an appointment, repair, class, or other service.
- `subscription`: a new or upgraded subscription or membership.
- `bill_payment`: a payment toward an existing bill or account balance.
- `digital_goods`: software, apps, e-books, in-game items, or digital gift cards.
- `other`: the evidence shows what was bought, and none of the types above fits.
- `unknown`: the evidence does not show what was bought.

Judge it from the checkout's own records: the `browser.pay` call and the checkout tool calls, the cart or order summary, the receipt, and screenshots. When the checkout stopped before those show the items, the task instructions can establish what it was buying. A flight and a stay booked together is `package`; any other combination takes the type that makes up most of the total.
