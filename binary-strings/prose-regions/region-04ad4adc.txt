Determines how search results are ranked.

- "informational" is the default (applied when omitted), and retrieves the most accurate results. For how-to, advice, tutorials, factual questions, reviews, comparisons, "best X", recipes, recommendations.
- "engagement" retrieves posts that have high engagement and are popular. For trending topics, opinions, "what are people saying", viral content, hot takes, debates, memes, reactions, community discussion, celebrity/gossip.
