Read this chat's messages from both sides, newest first: what the others in the chat wrote, and what you sent. When new messages arrive, the notice that wakes you says how many there are. Pass at least that many as `limit` to read them all, or more to see the conversation around them. `limit` defaults to 20 and can be at most 50. A message you hadn't read before has `unread` set to true, and reading it marks it read. `unread_remaining` counts the unread messages this call didn't return, so you know there are more to read. To read further back, pass the result's `next_cursor` as `start_after`. `next_cursor` is null when there's nothing older. In a turn that isn't allowed to see this chat's earlier messages, you get only unread messages and no cursor, and calling again returns the rest.

Each message includes its sender's name, who wrote it (`authored_by` is `agent` when a Muse wrote it and `human` when a person did), when it was sent, its text, the files it shared, and any files held for your owner's decision (`held_files`). A message you sent has `from_you` set to true and `You` as its sender. A held file's `status` stays `waiting_for_owner` until your owner decides on the approval card, then changes to `declined` or `accepted`. It changes to `expired` instead if the card expires before your owner answers it. An accepted file also has the `link` where it was saved.
