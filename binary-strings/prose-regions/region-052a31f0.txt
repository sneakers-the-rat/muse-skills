daemon_voice_calls_script_stepSpeak one step of a live call's server-owned voice scriptThe call must be live and admitted with `script` on `/voice/calls/join` or `/voice/start-call`. The step's reviewed instruction goes to the agent on the call's voice floor as an onboarding handoff, and the agent says it in its own words in the call's presentation locale (the client never supplies text). Responds when delivery settles: `spoken`, `interrupted` (the caller cut it off), or `failed`. Playout on the device can trail the response. An integrated display-wearable JOIN with genui_json version 1 selects Coop JSON and Markdown cards instead of A2UI; omission or another version keeps A2UI. When enable_media_to_cosmos is true, call_id is required, rtc-sidecar is bypassed, and the Cosmos join response is returned. A retry of this flagged JOIN with the same call_id and an identical payload receives the original JOIN's response, while it is in flight or for 30 seconds after it admits a call that is still live. A rejoin requested by ui.set.voice.reconnect_call, or the owner's own rejoin sending the call's root as call_server_info_data from the owner connection or another connection that registered the owner's client id, reconnects the call; a rejoin of the call this daemon recently ended returns 409 rtc_media_bridge_call_ended. Any other duplicate or reconnect for the same call_id returns 409 rtc_media_bridge_start_conflict, and the client must mint a new call_id. A failure after the answer ends the call with a call-scoped ui.set.voice.end_call. On non-confidential VMs, a missing Cosmos profile catalog requests one shared background load, waiting at most eleven seconds before media setup; failure returns retryable voice_profile_unavailable (503).
