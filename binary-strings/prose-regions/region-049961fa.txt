- "person": any human of any style (photo, 3D, cartoon, anime), including a human wearing a costume.

- "creature": a round, blob, fluffy or other compact creature or monster.
- "object": a single object or food item, with or without a face.
- "other": anything else.
photorealistic is true for a photograph or photorealistic render (of anything).
pose describes the BODY, not just the face, and is exactly one of:
- "frontal": the body itself is square-on to the camera: the chest or front of the body faces the viewer and the left and right sides of the body look roughly symmetric (a four-legged animal sitting or standing with its chest toward the camera, a person standing straight toward the camera, a round character seen from the front).
Be strict: call it "frontal" ONLY if the body's left and right halves are mirror images in pose: both front legs or both arms equally visible, shoulders and hips level, and no flank or side of the torso visible. A head turned toward the camera on a body that is angled, a tail or wing sweeping to one side with the body angled, one shoulder or hip closer to the camera, or a body seen slightly from the side all count as "three_quarter". When in doubt, choose "three_quarter".
- "three_quarter": the body is turned partly sideways (about 20 to 70 degrees), even if the face looks at the camera; for a four-legged animal, one flank of the body is visible.
- "side": the body is seen in profile.
- "back": the body is turned away.
- "lying_or_curled": lying down, curled up, or sprawled.

has_face is true if the character has a FACE: drawn, printed or sculpted eyes or a mouth that make it a character with a face. It is false for plain objects, devices, icons, boxes or shapes with no eyes and no mouth. When unsure, answer true.
sticks_out is true if the character has a TAIL, WINGS, or a FOUR-LEGGED stance (an animal standing or sitting on four legs) that sticks out to the side or behind and makes the whole figure much wider or longer than its head and upright body. It is false for upright characters whose outline is just head, body, arms and feet, even with big ears, hats or accessories on the head. When unsure, answer true.
head_accessory is true if a separate object or accessory sits on top of the character's head that is not part of its body: for example a hat, cap, crown, helmet, bow, flower, small animal, or toy on the head.
