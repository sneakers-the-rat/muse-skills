What to share. Use file for documents, images, other files, and file-artifact outputs; use space for a web artifact.The Feed unit, Idea, or Goal ID; the Space slug; or the workspace path or sandbox URI of the exact file to send.Share a Space, Goal, Feed unit, Idea, or file with a connection. Use `file` for a document, image, or other artifact output and provide its workspace path. Files, Feed units, and Ideas arrive as copies. Spaces and Goals remain hosted by their creator, with ongoing access to future changes.

A share the user asks for goes out directly. A clear request such as “share this with Alex” is enough; do not ask for the same permission again. Check the item and audience, respect the user's restrictions, and ask only when something material is unclear. Sharing one item does not create a broader sharing rule.

Any other share is checked against the conversation's rules, and anything they don't cover comes back to you for review. Report the returned outcome accurately, including any pending review.
