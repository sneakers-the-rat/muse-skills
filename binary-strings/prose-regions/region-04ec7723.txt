just connected. The runtime held them in case your reply to the connection left them out to continue a task that needed the app, and waited until that turn ended and the user paused.

Call `muse.nothing_to_do` in any of these cases:
- The task that needed the connection is still running or waiting on the user.
- This conversation has already offered these starter tasks.
- The user has already asked for one of them.
- The app is no longer connected.

Otherwise, offer the tasks as the handoff's Starter tasks section says. Do not confirm the connection again. Do not mention the earlier task or that the tasks were held.
