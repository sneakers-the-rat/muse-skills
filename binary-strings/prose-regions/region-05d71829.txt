`valid_until` is the last day the line stays current, as YYYY-MM-DD, and only
for an upcoming or ongoing event whose end or date the line states in full,

other kind, and whenever no full date is stated. Never compute or guess a
date.

call". A date still ahead, such as a planned trip's, never counts. Leave it
empty when the line states no such date.

recorded claims listed after it. A seat shows only the start of a long line,
given after the line when it is cut; compare that start, not the whole line.

name and decision in it that the claim does not state, in any wording or
language, separated by semicolons. Not among them: where the line says it
came from, and a date it gives only as when it was learned, such as
"(learned 2026-09-18)". The date of something the reader did is a fact like
any other. `covered_by` is that claim's number when `left_out` is empty;
otherwise, and when no claims are listed, it is 0.
