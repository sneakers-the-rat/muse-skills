Return each one as an object with `text` and `kind`, where kind is one of name, email, phone, place, handle or credential: names (for a greeting that contains a name, return the name), email addresses, phone numbers, street addresses, venues and other places, account names or handles, and passwords, keys or other credentials. Write `text` exactly as it appears in the screenshots, because it will be matched and replaced character for character.
