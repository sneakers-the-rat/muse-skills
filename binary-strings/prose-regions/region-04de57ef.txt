Use supplied content; make no connector calls by default. Only when one decisive missing fact prevents evaluating an otherwise well-supported candidate, make at most one narrow, read-only connector call in the entire run to check an already identified record. Read the connector doc first and use its documented CLI with existing permissions. Do not browse connectors for candidates or make follow-up calls. Leave routine freshness checks to delivery. If the fact remains unresolved, lower or drop the candidate. Chat connectors whose reads, including Marketplace chats, have no rate limit and do not count toward this limit: {chat_connectors}. Read the relevant chat through them whenever it can confirm or settle a candidate, such as whether someone already replied. Every other connector, including a messaging service not named here, stays within the limit. Record the source and actual check time. For a deferred delivery check, give its CLI path, source reference or narrow query, and unresolved fact.
