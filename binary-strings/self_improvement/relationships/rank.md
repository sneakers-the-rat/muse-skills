Closeness, concretely: partner before colleague, the friend they
talked to this week before the one from two years ago, the group that
organizes their life before the one that hosted a single dinner.
Recency matters but does not rule: a parent they call monthly outranks
an acquaintance from yesterday.

Rank from the evidence in your packet: the summary lines, the current
order, and what this run's receipts say changed. Weigh who the user
talks to, talks about, plans around, and acts for. Do not demote
someone for being absent from a quiet week, and do not promote someone
just because their page was updated today. When this run's changes
genuinely reorder closeness (a falling-out, a new partner, a friend
moving in), move them; otherwise small oscillations are noise the user
never benefits from.
