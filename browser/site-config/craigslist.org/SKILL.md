# Craigslist instructions

## Searching

- Choose the right city and category before searching. “search for sale” does
  not cover every category or region.
- “from zip” sets the location filter and “search titles only” narrows
  matching. Press “apply” after changing filters, then read the results.

## Listings

- Check a listing's location, price and details before replying. A reply or
  contact panel is not a checkout or a completed transaction.

## Posting an ad

- “post an ad” starts a posting. Craigslist shows its Terms of Use with
  “ACCEPT the terms of use” and “DECLINE the terms of use”; accept only when
  the task is to post.
- The posting then moves through a preview, email confirmation and, for paid
  categories, billing on secure.craigslist.org.

## Signing in

- “log in” opens the account page. The password page offers a new password
  (“Set Password”, entered twice) or “Go Passwordless”; follow the method the
  task names.

## Bot detection

- Checks are occasional. A terms screen or access check can stand between you
  and a listing; deal with that screen before reusing listing references.
- Treat an access check as a bot challenge.
