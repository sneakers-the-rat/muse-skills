# Loblaws instructions

## Store and fulfilment

- Choose the store (“View location: …” or the Store Locator) and pickup or
  delivery before searching. Prices, stock and time slots depend on that
  selection.
- PC Express is the online pickup and delivery service; its charges are under
  “Delivery Fees” and “Pickup Fees”.

## Products and cart

- Search with “Search for product” and “Submit Search”. Check the pack size,
  unit price and quantity before “Add … to cart”.
- Review the cart for quantities and substitution preferences. Weight-based
  items carry estimated totals that can change at fulfilment.

## Checkout

- Signing in, choosing a time slot and submitting the order are separate
  steps. Confirm the final order state and the selected pickup or delivery
  time.

## Bot detection

- Loblaws often blocks automated visits with an “Access Denied” page (“You
  don't have permission to access … on this server.”) and a Reference number.
  It is a bot wall, not empty inventory.
- Treat it as a bot challenge. Repeating product searches does not clear it.
