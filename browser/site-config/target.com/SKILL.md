# Target instructions

## Page setup

- The header shows the ship-to ZIP (“Ship to location: …”) and the store
  (“Store: …”). Set the store or delivery destination before checking stock;
  product pages distinguish “Pickup”, “Shipping” and “Delivery”.

## Searching and product pages

- Search with “What can we help you find? suggestions appear below”; some views
  expose it as “searchTerm”. Choose the item's variant before “Add to cart”.
- Registry, wish-list and favorite controls do not add the item to the cart.

## Cart

- Close the add-to-cart panel, then read the cart count and the line quantity.
- Some shipping items carry an order minimum, shown on the item page and in the
  cart (“Only ships with $35 orders”); the checkout button then notes how much
  more qualifies for shipping.

## Signing in

1. “Sign in or create account” asks for “Email or mobile phone”, then
   “Continue”. “Sign in to check out” in the cart leads here.
2. The next page offers “Enter your password”, “Get a code” and “Use a
   passkey”. Choose the method the task supports.

## Bot detection

- Target frequently interrupts browsing with “Quick verification”: “Press &
  hold to confirm you're a human (and not a bot).” and a “Press & Hold Human
  Challenge” button, sometimes before the homepage loads.
- Treat it as a bot challenge. Repeated product navigation while it is showing
  does not prove an item is unavailable.
