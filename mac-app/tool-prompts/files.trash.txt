Permanently delete Mac files or folders; this cannot be undone. Use files.trash instead unless the user explicitly asks for permanent deletion. ok means at least one item was removed, so check failed_count and results. permission_required means macOS withheld every path; permission_denied_paths lists any it withheld.

Prefer files commands when they satisfy the task. For Finder work, use files.search, files.list, files.rename, files.trash and open_app file_path rather than Finder's Find, inline rename or Move to Trash, which are slow to verify and can bring Finder to the front.

The macOS Trash is off-limits to the agent, so its contents can't be listed, read, changed, moved, or deleted. Use files.trash to move a file into the Trash (recoverable); reaching into the Trash itself, or emptying it, is refused.
