Find Mac files and folders by name, kind, extension, Finder tag, size or date, searching subfolders unless recursive=false. For a plain listing of one folder, use files.list. Filters combine with AND and match names and metadata only (no file contents, no Spotlight). Hidden files, credentials, Trash and connector-owned data are skipped. Results are sorted, then paged: pass next_offset with identical filters and sort. partial=true (see partial_reason) means the scan stopped early, skipped entries or lacked metadata, so counts and order may be incomplete; narrow the path or filters before claiming the newest, the largest, or that nothing matches. To open a result, use computer.control open_app file_path=<path>.

Finder's Find (Command-F) brings Finder to the front over the user's screen, so nothing was sent. Find files with files.search or files.list instead, then use open_app with file_path, or select the item by element_number.

Prefer files commands when they satisfy the task. For Finder work, use files.search, files.list, files.rename, files.trash and open_app file_path rather than Finder's Find, inline rename or Move to Trash, which are slow to verify and can bring Finder to the front.
