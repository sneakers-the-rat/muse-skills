# Other flight sources

- Use this guide when the user wants more coverage, or the connectors and Google Flights leave a gap.

## Websites

- [Google Flights](https://www.google.com/travel/flights) is the fallback when the connectors may be missing something or the user asks. [Flight comparison](flights.md) describes when.

- [Kayak](https://www.kayak.com/flights) and [Skyscanner](https://www.skyscanner.com/) are extra checks only when the user asks for more coverage.

- The operating airline's own site shows member fares, credits and full fare rules, and it services the booking afterward.

- Regional and low-cost carriers can be missing from comparison sites or cheaper on their own sites, such as [Ryanair](https://www.ryanair.com/), [easyJet](https://www.easyjet.com/) and [Wizz Air](https://wizzair.com/) in Europe, [Volaris](https://www.volaris.com/) and [Viva Aerobus](https://www.vivaaerobus.com/) in Mexico, [Porter](https://www.flyporter.com/) in Canada and [Peach](https://www.flypeach.com/) in Japan. Add their bag and seat fees, which these fares often leave out.

- When the user asks for more coverage, [Booking.com Flights](https://www.booking.com/flights/) is another seller to compare. Check who issues the ticket and who handles changes.

- When the user asks for more coverage, [ITA Matrix](https://matrix.itasoftware.com/search) explores complex routings and fare rules. It does not sell tickets, so take its candidates to a seller.

## Alternatives for better results

- Widen dates, airports or the order of cities when the user allows it. Count the extra ground transfer, hotel night or bag fee before calling the result cheaper.

- Check member fares, credits, companion certificates and points when the user has them. Compare points with cash on live award availability, taxes and transfer rules; a points transfer is usually irreversible, so it follows a confirmed award rather than a check.

- Try the airline's site for the departure country when it may price differently, and check that a resident-only or local-payment fare is open to this traveler.

- Book with the airline when flexibility or support matters more than a small saving, since it handles changes and disruptions for its own bookings.
