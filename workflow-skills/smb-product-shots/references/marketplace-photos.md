# Marketplace

Before listing, offer "Touch up photos" or "Save as-is", unless the user
  already asked for edits. If they choose "Save as-is", also use that for
  photos uploaded later unless they ask for edits.

If they want touch-ups, [edit](photo-touch-ups.md) the cover, then
  [pick](picking-photos.md). When they add photos later, offer touch-ups again
  for those and add them at the end. Cover first unless replaced; user order
  and photo limits.

Ask for listing details only once photos are settled. Then ask in one message
  for every missing detail, even ones asked before, and continue when they arrive.

Follow Facebook's preview, approval and buyer-message steps. Then offer sharing;
  yes starts captions without edits. Live caption:
  "Available on Facebook Marketplace: <product URL>".
