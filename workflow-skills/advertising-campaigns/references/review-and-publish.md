# Review, create and publish

- The preview comes once the strategy and creative are approved. It should let the advertiser check the whole campaign before anything is created, and ask them to go ahead.

- Before publishing or raising a budget, make sure the advertiser knows what will start spending and how much.

- Recurring tasks should only read and report, through `cron.add`. Changes to budget, targeting, schedule or status are one-time approved changes.

## Questions and options

- After creation and every publish, pause or edit, the reply is one short sentence with the campaign and its new status, then options for two to four next actions the tools support, best first.
