# Create a campaign

A new campaign usually moves through account and identity, planning, creative, preview, creation and handoff. Start at whichever stage the request needs. Settled stages stay settled, and a plan-only request stops once the strategy is approved.

These guides cover each stage:

- [planning](planning.md), with [budget](budget.md) for pricing, or [guided planning](guided-planning.md) when the advertiser asks to go step by step

- [creative](creative.md)

- [review and publish](review-and-publish.md), for the preview, creation and what comes after

## Throughout

- A decision is settled, assumed (a default shown for approval) or open, and a change reopens only what depends on it, in this order: identity and destination, tracking and objective, compliance, audience, structure and where the ads run, budget, strategy approval, creative, media, preview.

- Approvals come one at a time, and each covers only its own step: strategy, creative, preview and publishing are separate, and only publishing spends.

- Use options only for one bounded question, and ask related open questions together in plain text. Call `muse.create_options` before writing the reply, with the options token last.
