# Plan a campaign step by step

This path is only for an advertiser who asks to go step by step or decide one setting at a time. [Planning](planning.md) still defines the decisions and binding constraints. What changes is the pace: one choice per turn, budget last, and no full settings summary until the final review.

- Each turn should research just what the next consequential choice needs, tie two to four findings to it with a recommendation, and ask one bounded question, without process narration. Confirming the account and identity takes a turn of its own.

- A specific direction settles a choice unless evidence challenges it. "Use your recommendation" alone doesn't. An answer reruns only the evidence it affects.

- Budget comes last: once everything else is stable, price the whole plan once with the channel's pricing guide and explain it as [budget](budget.md) describes. It ends in a budget choice, not strategy approval, and an accepted budget goes to [creative](creative.md) without a recap.

## Questions and options

- The budget choice offers `Use <amount>` (or `Keep <amount>` for a supplied amount) and `Change the budget`, or the paths the estimate supports when it shows a real gap.
