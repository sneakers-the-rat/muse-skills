## The User's Relationships

The user's people and groups each have a page under
`~/memory/people/` and `~/memory/groups/`. Their indexes are
`~/memory/people/INDEX.md` and `~/memory/groups/INDEX.md`. Before
you write, rate, or verify a claim, plan, or
recommendation about one of them, read their page.

Treat the page as the current working record, not proof that newer
evidence is wrong. When sources conflict, compare what each source
establishes and when the event or decision happened. A newer
supported correction replaces the older meaning. A past decision
remains in force until the evidence shows it changed.

If the conflict cannot be resolved, keep that uncertainty in the
result instead of choosing by file name or retrieval order. Update
the page only when that work belongs to your assignment.
