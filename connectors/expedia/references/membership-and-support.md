# Expedia membership and support

- These are Expedia's terms for its U.S. One Key program, for answering a user's questions about Expedia. Where to book a given stay still comes from comparing live prices and terms. The [Expedia Help Center](https://www.expedia.com/helpcenter/) has current details and anything not covered here.

## One Key

- One Key is Expedia's free loyalty program. Members get instant discounts at select hotels: 10% for Members, 15% for Silver, and 20% for Gold and Platinum.

- Through Muse, member prices, OneKeyCash and Trip Elements apply when the user's Expedia account is connected, as on Expedia.com.

- Tiers come from Trip Elements collected in a calendar year: 5 for Silver, 15 for Gold and 30 for Platinum. Each eligible hotel night of at least US$25 before taxes and fees counts as one, and the count resets each January 1.

## OneKeyCash

- Members earn it on eligible non-flight bookings, more at higher tiers. $1 in OneKeyCash is $1 toward eligible travel on Expedia and participating brands, with no blackout dates. It isn't redeemable for cash.

- It arrives after the trip is complete, or up to 45 days later for Pay Later stays. Cancelled bookings earn none.

- It doesn't expire while the member books or redeems at least once every 18 months. Promotional balances can expire sooner.

- Muse can't show or apply a OneKeyCash balance. Members check and redeem it at checkout for eligible Pay Now travel on Expedia.com or in the Expedia app.

## Prices, cancellations and support

- Hotel prices include taxes and fees, and charges collected at the property are shown separately.

- Cancellation terms belong to each rate. Free cancellation applies only where the rate offers it and before its deadline, which some hotels set more than 24 hours before check-in. Pay Later doesn't by itself mean free cancellation.

- Changes and cancellations happen in Trips on Expedia.com or the Expedia app, where support is available 24/7 through a virtual agent and live agents. Higher tiers get priority routing.
