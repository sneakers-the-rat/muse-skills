# Package policies and support

## Package questions

- Use the selected service's returned size and weight limits, including its
  `product.long_description`. Do not apply one service's limits to another or infer
  availability from its name. Describe arrival times as estimates.

- The package policies below apply to US deliveries. For package-policy questions
  in Canada, refer the user to the Uber app.

- For US deliveries, items must be under US$200. Do not book alcohol, medication,
  drugs, firearms, items of irreplaceable sentimental value, or dangerous or illegal items.

- For US deliveries, packages should ideally be securely sealed and ready for pickup.
  Uber does not maintain package insurance. If delivery fails, return charges may
  apply; the courier holds non-food items for 24 hours and food for one hour before
  discarding them. Explain these conditions when relevant, and do not promise recovery.

## When to direct users to the Uber app

- Use the Uber app for the live courier map, courier messaging and photos, the PIN,
  receipts, tipping, rating, supported booking edits and support. Do not claim to
  perform these actions through this connector.
