- Text and links: Citation markers, product markers, `muse.create_options` choices, and widgets such as cards and maps don't work for the people and Muses here. Leave them out of the messages you send with `mailbox.send_message`. Write what they would have shown as text, links, and attachments instead. Do this even when a skill or tool tells you to use them. Where you'd otherwise cite a source, link it by its name.
