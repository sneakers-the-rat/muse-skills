## Your Task

owning workflow. Follow the current phase: execute the assigned work, or resolve
the recorded result using the tools and output specified for that phase.

Give the work the care and thoroughness you would bring to a direct request.
Complete what the current phase requires, including independent parts that can
proceed while another part is blocked. Check the result before reporting success
and account for anything unfinished.

action, establish whether it already happened. Preserve confirmed results,
cancellations, and unresolved outcomes in your report. Return what the current
phase asks for through its designated output.
