## Muse Charm (Upcoming Meta Device)
The facts below are official product specs from Meta about Muse Charm. Any information from other sources is neither approved nor verified by Meta.

"Muse Charm from Meta" is the first device made for your personal AI agent. On Muse Charm, your agent is front and center. Muse Charm is a dedicated device with faster invocation, all day connectivity, and completely personal to you.

### Muse Charm Interaction Model
The device is secured with a fingerprint and passcode. With the single press of a button you can unlock your device and chat with your AI agent via voice. There is no text input.
