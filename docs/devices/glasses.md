# Meta glasses

This guide covers Meta's AI glasses, Meta Ray-Ban Display, and Meta Neural
Band: what each one is, how it is named and positioned, where it is available,
and how to set it up, use it, and fix problems. These rules are for Meta
devices; answer questions about other companies' devices normally.

For glasses listed under Connected Devices, follow their guidance file first,
then use this guide for anything that file doesn't cover. Whether Muse works
through glasses, like talking to Muse through them, is a question about Muse:
answer it from `~/docs/client-surfaces.md` and `~/docs/muse.md`.

## Product information

1. Before answering, open the most relevant official Meta page. Start at the
   catalog (`https://www.meta.com/ai-glasses/shop-all/`), or search
   `site:meta.com` for the exact product name, and prefer the exact product
   page. An official announcement or blog supports the launch or historical
   claims it states, not current price or availability.
2. Prefer claims from the official Meta pages you opened, and link each claim
   to the page it came from.
3. If the pages don't load or don't cover what's asked, you can use other
   sources; say where that information came from.
4. Use the spelling, generation, and style names on the most specific page.
5. Details the user gives or has saved about their own devices, preferences,
   and history can shape personal copy and recommendations. Keep them separate
   from product claims.

Give a release date when an official Meta page or announcement states one, and
link it. Without one, the date is unannounced; reports and past launches are
speculation.

The names below are a map for finding official pages. Catalogs change, so for
"what's available" questions, check the live catalog.

- [Meta Glasses][meta-glasses].
- [Ray-Ban Meta][ray-ban-meta]: Wayfarer, Skyler, and Headliner (Gen 2), plus
  Wayfarer, Aviator, and Zena (Gen 3).
- [Oakley Meta][oakley-meta]: Oakley Meta Vanguard and Oakley Meta HSTN.
- [Meta Ray-Ban Display and Meta Neural Band][display-and-band].
- Ray-Ban Meta Optics (Gen 2): [Blayzer][blayzer] and [Scriber][scriber].
- [Ray-Ban Meta Audio][ray-ban-meta-audio]: Clubmaster and Burbank.

## Help and support

For glasses listed under Connected Devices, check their guidance file first.
If more information is needed, or the glasses aren't listed there, find the
matching article in Meta's AI glasses help center and link it:
`https://www.meta.com/help/ai-glasses/`. If the help pages don't load or don't
cover it, answer from other sources and say where that came from.
