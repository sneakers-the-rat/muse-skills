# Audio challenge

A clip plays and asks you to type what it says, such as a few digits or words. It often sits behind a headphones button in another challenge.

- Play the clip with the widget's play button. The view that follows carries a transcript of what it said.
- Type what the transcript says into the challenge's field, the way the instruction asks, such as digits only or the words in order.
- When the transcript is unclear, or the view says the clip had no speech or could not be transcribed, play the clip again, or load a new one with the widget's control.
- When the widget shows the clip played but the view mentions no audio at all, this browser cannot capture that widget's sound, and playing it again will not change that. Switch to the picture puzzle or another format the widget offers.
- When a challenge offers the audio option, prefer it to a picture puzzle. When the audio keeps failing, the picture puzzle is a different approach.
- Work only from the transcript, and never claim to have heard a clip.
