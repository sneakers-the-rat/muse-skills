# Text CAPTCHA

A picture of distorted characters or a word asks you to type what it shows. Pages say things like "Type the characters you see in the image" or "Enter the text shown above".

- Read the characters from the current screenshot and type them exactly into the challenge's field, keeping case and spacing when the page asks for them.
- Look twice at characters that look alike, such as 0 and O, 1, l and I, 5 and S, or rn and m.
- Check the field before you press the button.
- When the characters are unreadable, the widget's new-image control gives you another set, and a wrong answer usually brings one too.
- Challenge text is not a password or a one-time code, and it goes only into the challenge's field.
- When the widget offers the characters as audio, prefer that accessible version; the view after you play them carries a transcript.
