# Game puzzle

A matching game runs over several rounds, played with arrow buttons or by picking one picture. Pages say things like "Use the arrows to match the image on the left" or "Pick the image that follows the pattern".

- When the widget offers an accessible format, such as audio, prefer it to the game.
- Read the instruction and the round counter, such as "2 of 5", before each round. The goal can change between rounds.
- With arrows, compare the current picture with the goal, click the arrow that brings it closer, and check the picture in each view.
- When clicking an arrow does not change the picture, click the picture once, then `press` ArrowRight or ArrowLeft with no target.
- Press the widget's submit button as soon as the picture matches, without first cycling through every option; a long survey can let the page's session expire.
- With picks, choose the one picture that satisfies the instruction, describing it by what it shows and where it sits.
- A wrong round usually restarts the set. Read the new rounds afresh.
- Finish every round, then check the page; a completion animation does not prove the page is usable.
